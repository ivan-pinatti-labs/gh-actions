# .github/workflows/sonarqube.yml with --only-binary=:all:.

# A floor resolves to whatever is newest on PyPI when the job runs, which puts
# the seven day `minimumReleaseAge` in .github/renovate.json5 out of the loop:
# that window governs when Renovate proposes a change, not what pip installs.
# An exact pin is also the only shape the `requirement_line` grammar in
# .github/pin-only.yml recognizes, so a bump can take the unattended lane.
#

# writes the report SonarQube Cloud reads. pyyaml is imported by the suite and
# by src/pin_only.py.
